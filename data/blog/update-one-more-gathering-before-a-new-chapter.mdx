Brothers,

Our upcoming meeting(11/12) is canceled and will be moved to next Thursday(11/19). I must go out of town for a family matter. Next Thursday will also be our final in-person gathering together before my family and I move to Arizona.

That’s a hard sentence to write. This group has meant so much to me; the conversations, the prayers, the honesty, and the opportunity to walk alongside you as we grow in Christ. Thank you for showing up, sharing your lives, and making room for one another.

I fully believe this move is a call from the Lord Himself. I don’t know everything He has ahead for us, but I trust the One leading us. I’m stepping forward in faith while carrying a deep love and gratitude for the brothers He has placed in my life here.

My prayer is that you remain safe, comforted, and connected to the Lord and to each other. Keep checking in. Keep praying for one another. Keep making room for honest conversations, especially when life gets heavy. The relationships we’ve built are worth continuing to invest in.

Next Thursday(11/19), I’d love for us to spend time together, pray for one another, and thank God for what He has done among us.
